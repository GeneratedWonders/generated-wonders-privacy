1. Introduction

Generated Wonders respects your privacy and is committed to providing a transparent experience regarding how information is handled.

ROLL//ONE is an offline 3D dice simulator featuring a virtual dice tower, physics-based dice rolling, and a collection of classic tabletop dice.

The application is designed to function without requiring personal information, user accounts, or an internet connection.

This Privacy Policy describes the information handled by ROLL//ONE and the choices available to users.

2. Information We Collect

ROLL//ONE does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with Generated Wonders or third parties.

The application does not use:

  • User accounts or registration systems.
  • Advertising networks.
  • Analytics or tracking services.
  • Third-party data collection services.
  • Location tracking.
  • Cloud-based user profiles.

Generated Wonders does not receive users' dice-roll history or application preferences.

3. Locally Stored Information

ROLL//ONE stores certain information locally on your device to provide its core features.

This information may include:

  • Dice-roll history and results.
  • Selected dice and dice quantities.
  • Application preferences and settings.
  • Audio preferences.
  • Haptic feedback preferences.
  • Performance and display preferences.

This information remains within the application's local storage and is not transmitted to Generated Wonders.

Users can clear their saved dice-roll history through the application's History controls.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until it is cleared through the application, removed through your device's application settings, or deleted when the application is uninstalled.

Actual removal may depend on your device's operating system and any applicable device backup or restoration settings.

Because Generated Wonders does not receive or maintain users' dice-roll history on its servers, we do not maintain a remote database of this information.

5. Device Permissions

ROLL//ONE uses Android's vibration functionality to provide optional haptic feedback during dice rolling and application interactions.

Haptic feedback can be enabled or disabled within the application's settings.

ROLL//ONE does not require access to your:

  • Camera.
  • Microphone.
  • Contacts.
  • Precise location.
  • Personal files or photographs.

6. Internet Connectivity

ROLL//ONE is designed to function offline.

An internet connection is not required to roll dice, access saved history, or use the application's core features.

The application does not require an online account or connection to a Generated Wonders server.

7. Advertising and In-App Purchases

ROLL//ONE does not contain third-party advertisements, advertising trackers, or in-app purchases.

The application does not use personal information for targeted advertising.

8. Children's Privacy

ROLL//ONE does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

The application does not provide user accounts, social networking features, personalized advertisements, or online communication between users.

9. Data Security

Application settings and dice-roll history are stored locally on your device.

Generated Wonders does not operate a server that receives or stores this information.

Users can manage locally stored information through the application's available controls and their device's operating system.

10. Third-Party Services

ROLL//ONE does not integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking services.

When the application is downloaded through Google Play, Google's own platform services, including downloads, purchases where applicable, updates, and platform security, are governed by Google's separate privacy policies.

For information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Generated Wonders may update this Privacy Policy if the application's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically for updates.
